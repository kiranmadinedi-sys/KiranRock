# KiranRock AI Trading – Functional Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
KiranRock AI Trading is an advanced, fully-automated trading platform leveraging a 5-tier market universe and real-time AI analysis to deliver institutional-grade trading intelligence to all users. The platform is designed for both desktop and mobile, with a focus on transparency, risk management, and user empowerment.

---

## 2. Key Features

### 2.1. 5-Tier Market Universe
- **Tier 1:** ETFs (broad market, sector, innovation, leveraged)
- **Tier 2:** Mega-cap momentum (S&P500, NASDAQ leaders)
- **Tier 3:** Earnings watch (companies reporting in 5–20 days)
- **Tier 4:** Mid-cap breakouts (diversified, high-growth)
- **Tier 5:** Speculative/emerging (biotech, AI, space tech)

### 2.2. Real-Time AI Analysis
- Scans the market every 5 minutes.
- Scores and ranks stocks using technicals, news, and proprietary signals.
- Only top opportunities are surfaced for trading.

### 2.3. Smart Filtering & Risk Management
- Advanced pre-filters eliminate low-quality, illiquid, or risky stocks.
- Automated stop-loss, sector allocation, and position sizing.
- Circuit breakers for market volatility and loss limits.

### 2.4. User Experience
- **Paper Trading Mode:** Safe simulation for all users (no real money at risk).
- **Live Trading Launch:** Scheduled for 15 days from today, with early access offers.
- **Mobile-Ready:** Responsive design for all devices.
- **Executive Presentation:** Marketing page with animated video, energetic male narration, and interactive pitch.

## 3. User Benefits

## 4. Technical Highlights

- All enhancements are backward-compatible and additive—no breaking changes to architecture.
