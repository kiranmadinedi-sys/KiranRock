1. Executive Summary

KiranRock AI Trading is an advanced, fully-automated trading platform leveraging a 5-tier market universe and real-time AI analysis to deliver institutional-grade trading intelligence to all users. The platform is designed for both desktop and mobile, with a focus on transparency, risk management, and user empowerment.

2. Key Features

  • 5-Tier Market Universe: ETFs, mega-cap momentum, earnings watch, mid-cap breakouts, speculative/emerging.
  • Real-Time AI Analysis: Scans the market every 5 minutes, scores and ranks stocks using technicals, news, and proprietary signals.
  • Smart Filtering & Risk Management: Advanced pre-filters, automated stop-loss, sector allocation, position sizing, and circuit breakers.
  • User Experience: Paper trading mode, live trading launch in 15 days, mobile-ready, executive presentation with video and narration.

3. User Benefits

  • AI-Powered Edge: Outperform traditional screening with institutional-grade intelligence.
  • Fully Automated: No manual intervention required; AI handles end-to-end trade selection and execution.
  • Transparency: Users can see every trade, score, and decision rationale.
  • Security: Strict risk controls and exposure limits.
  • Early Access Offers: Incentives for early adopters and priority onboarding.

4. Technical Highlights

  • Node.js backend with modular services for market screening, AI scoring, and trade execution.
  • Frontend supports public marketing pages and authenticated dashboards.
  • All enhancements are backward-compatible and additive—no breaking changes to architecture.
  • Presentation layer includes both video and TTS/voiceover for executive communication.

5. Go-Live Plan
